We do not receive or store your card number, card security code or mobile money PIN; Paystack handles these directly.

Location. The app asks for location permission ("while using the app") the first time you go online. Your position is used only while you are on duty, to show you on the map and to open turn-by-turn directions. The app does not track you in the background or when you are offline, and it does not currently send your position to our servers. When live order dispatch launches, your on-duty position will be shared with our dispatch system to offer you nearby orders; we will update this policy before that happens.

Documents. Photos and PDFs of your documents are kept in private storage. Only you and authorised GhanaDash verification staff can open them, and we use them only to confirm your identity and that you are allowed and insured to ride.

2. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To confirm that the mobile number on your rider profile belongs to you.
  • To verify your identity, licence, roadworthiness and insurance from the documents you upload.
  • To show your position on the map and guide you while you are on duty.
  • To review and approve rider applications, and to keep a record of those decisions.
  • To run your free trial and weekly subscription and to verify your payments.
  • To contact you about your account, deliveries and service changes.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse (for example, limiting repeated sign-in or code requests) and to meet legal obligations.

We rely on performing our contract with you, our legitimate interest in running a safe service, your consent where we ask for it, and legal obligations. We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. SMS verification codes

When you add your mobile number in the Rider app, we send a one-time 6-digit code by SMS from the sender ID GhanaDash through our SMS provider, mNotify. Codes expire after 10 minutes, work only once and are stored by us only in hashed form until used or expired. We send these codes only when you request one, never for marketing. We will never ask you to tell us a code by phone or message.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers who process it for us, as needed:

  • Supabase: database, account authentication and private storage of your documents.
  • Google: sign-in, if you choose "Continue with Google"; maps on Android and turn-by-turn directions in Google Maps.
  • Apple: maps on iPhone (Apple Maps).
  • Paystack: subscription payments.
  • mNotify (BMS Africa): delivery of SMS verification codes (your phone number and the message).
  • Cloudflare: hosting and security for this website.

We may also disclose data where required by Ghanaian law or a lawful request from authorities, or to protect the safety of riders, customers or the public.

5. Where data is stored

Some of our providers store or process data outside Ghana. Where this happens we use providers that apply appropriate security safeguards, as required by Act 843.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account, rider profile and compliance documents: while your account is open, and up to 12 months after it is closed, unless you ask us to delete them sooner and we have no legal reason to keep them.
  • Payment and subscription records: 6 years, to meet tax and accounting obligations.
  • Approval decisions and account audit logs: for as long as the related account records are kept.
  • Unused SMS verification codes: deleted within 10 minutes.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to rider data is restricted to authorised staff, sign-in sessions use signed tokens, verification codes are hashed and rate-limited, and we keep audit records of administrative actions. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will notify you and the Data Protection Commission as the law requires.

8. Your rights

Under Act 843 you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data, or stop or limit how we use it, where the law allows;
  • withdraw consent you previously gave.

Email us at the address below; we will respond within the time the law requires. If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to Ghana's Data Protection Commission.

9. Age requirement

GhanaDash rider accounts are for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

We will post any changes here with a new effective date, and tell riders in the app about significant changes.
