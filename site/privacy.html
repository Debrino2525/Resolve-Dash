2. Location

Both apps ask for location permission "while using the app" only. Neither app tracks you in the background or after you close it.

  • Customers. When you book, the app reads your position once to suggest your pickup point, show riders near you and list nearby places first in search. It is not tracked afterwards. The pickup and drop-off points you confirm are stored with the delivery.
  • Riders, while online. While you are online in the Rider app, your position is sent to our servers regularly so we can offer you nearby delivery requests. When you go offline, sending stops.
  • Riders, during a delivery. From the moment you accept a delivery until it is completed or cancelled, the customer of that delivery can see your live position on their tracking map.
  • Riders near a customer. When customers open the app, they see how many riders are online nearby, shown at an approximate position (rounded to about 100 metres), without names.

3. Photos and chat

  • Package photo (optional). When you book, you can add a photo of the item. It is stored privately and shown only to you, the rider who takes the delivery, and authorised GhanaDash staff handling a question or claim about it.
  • Chat. While a delivery is in progress, the customer and the assigned rider can send each other messages and photos in the app. Only those two people and authorised GhanaDash staff (for safety, support or disputes) can read them. Chat closes when the delivery ends; you can still read past messages.
  • Rider documents. Photos and PDFs of rider documents are kept in private storage. Only the rider and authorised GhanaDash verification staff can open them, and we use them only to confirm identity and that the rider is allowed and insured to ride.
  • Photos are resized and compressed on your phone before upload. The apps only use the camera or photo library when you choose to add a photo.

4. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To take, price and dispatch your deliveries, match them with a nearby rider and show their progress.
  • To let the customer and rider of a delivery contact each other by phone, WhatsApp or in-app chat.
  • To confirm handover with a delivery PIN and record completed deliveries, ratings and cash fares.
  • To find places when you search, and to suggest your recent pickup and drop-off places.
  • To verify riders' identity, licence, roadworthiness and insurance, and to keep a record of approval decisions.
  • To run riders' free trial and subscription and to verify their payments.
  • To provide support, handle complaints, claims and disputes, and keep riders and customers safe.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse (for example, limiting repeated sign-in or code requests) and to meet legal obligations.

We rely on performing our contract with you, our legitimate interest in running a safe service, your consent where we ask for it (such as location and camera access), and legal obligations. We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

5. SMS sign-in and verification codes

When you sign in or sign up with your phone number, or confirm a number in the app, we send a one-time 6-digit code by SMS from the sender ID GhanaDash through our SMS provider, mNotify. Codes expire after 10 minutes and work only once. We send codes only when you request one, never for marketing, and we limit how many codes a number can receive. We will never ask you to tell us a code by phone or message.

6. What customers and riders see about each other

  • Riders offered your delivery see the pickup and drop-off addresses, pins and landmarks, the package type, whether you added a photo, the distance and the fare, so they can decide whether to accept. They do not see names or phone numbers at this stage.
  • The rider who accepts your delivery also sees your name, the contact names and phone numbers you entered, your notes and the package photo, until the delivery ends. Riders never see the delivery PIN.
  • The customer of a delivery sees the assigned rider's name, phone number, motorbike registration, rating, number of completed trips and live position until the delivery ends.

7. Service providers

We share personal data only with service providers who process it for us, as needed:

  • Supabase: database, account sign-in and private storage of photos and documents.
  • Render: hosting of our application servers.
  • Cloudflare: hosting and security for this website and our admin tools.
  • mNotify (BMS Africa): delivery of SMS codes (your phone number and the message).
  • Mapbox: maps, road distance for pricing, routes and turning a map pin into an address (map coordinates).
  • OpenStreetMap search (Photon, operated by komoot) and HERE: finding places from the words you type, with your approximate position.
  • Google: sign-in, if you choose "Continue with Google"; maps on Android and turn-by-turn directions if you open Google Maps.
  • Apple: sign-in, if you choose "Continue with Apple"; maps on iPhone.
  • Paystack: rider subscription payments.
  • WhatsApp: only if you choose to contact someone through WhatsApp, which opens the WhatsApp app under its own terms.

We may also disclose data where required by Ghanaian law or a lawful request from authorities, or to protect the safety of riders, customers or the public.

8. Where data is stored

Some of our providers store or process data outside Ghana, including in the European Union. Where this happens we use providers that apply appropriate security safeguards, as required by Act 843.

9. How long we keep it

  • Accounts and profiles: while your account is open, and up to 12 months after it is closed, unless you ask us to delete them sooner and we have no legal reason to keep them.
  • Delivery records (addresses, contacts, fare, status, rating): up to 6 years, for accounting, tax and dispute handling.
  • Package photos, chat messages and chat photos: up to 12 months after the delivery, so we can handle questions and claims.
  • Rider compliance documents: while the rider account is open, and up to 12 months after it is closed.
  • Payment and subscription records: 6 years, to meet tax and accounting obligations.
  • Rider position: only the latest position is kept while a rider is online; it is not kept as a history.
  • Approval decisions and admin audit logs: for as long as the related account records are kept.
  • Unused SMS codes: expire after 10 minutes.

10. Data kept only on your phone

To make signing back in quicker, the apps remember the first name and phone number of the last person who signed in on that phone, in the phone's encrypted storage. It is never sent to us and you can remove it with "Use another account". Your sign-in session is also stored in the phone's encrypted storage.

11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Photos and documents are kept in private storage and shown only to the people described above. Access to personal data is restricted to authorised staff, admin tools are protected by an additional sign-in, sign-in sessions use signed tokens, codes are rate-limited, and we keep audit records of administrative actions. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will notify you and the Data Protection Commission as the law requires.

12. Your rights

Under Act 843 you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct inaccurate data (you can also edit your name and email in the app's Account page);
  • delete your data, or stop or limit how we use it, where the law allows;
  • withdraw consent you previously gave (for example, turn off location or camera access in your phone's settings).

Email us at the address below; we will respond within the time the law requires. If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to Ghana's Data Protection Commission.

13. Age requirement

GhanaDash accounts are for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

14. Changes

We will post any changes here with a new effective date, and tell you in the apps about significant changes.
