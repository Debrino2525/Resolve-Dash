5. Sending packages (customers)

Booking and price

  • You must be at least 18 and have a Ghana mobile number that you confirm by SMS. One account per person.
  • Before you book, the app shows the fare for your route, based on road distance. That is the price you pay; it is held for 10 minutes while you confirm.
  • Place the pickup and drop-off pins on the exact spots and give correct contact numbers. If the rider can't find the place or reach anyone, the delivery may be delayed or cancelled.
  • You can have up to 3 deliveries in progress at a time.

Paying

  • Deliveries are cash on delivery: the fare is paid in cash to the rider when the package is delivered, unless the app says otherwise.
  • Riders are independent operators. GhanaDash does not ask for card, mobile money or PIN details for cash deliveries.

Handover PIN

  • Each delivery has a 4-digit PIN shown only in your app. Give it to the person receiving the package. The rider enters it to complete the delivery, which proves the right person received it.
  • Never share the PIN before the package arrives. After several wrong PIN attempts the delivery is held and our team will contact you.

Cancelling

  • You can cancel free of charge in the app while we are finding a rider or before the rider has collected the package.
  • Once the rider has the package, it can't be cancelled in the app. Contact us and we will help return or redirect it.
  • If no rider is available, the delivery is cancelled and you are not charged.

What you can't send

You must not send, and riders may refuse:

  • anything illegal, including drugs, weapons, ammunition and stolen or counterfeit goods;
  • dangerous items, including fuel, gas cylinders, explosives, fireworks and corrosive or poisonous substances;
  • cash, gold, jewellery and other high-value items;
  • live animals, people's documents you have no right to send, or anything too big or heavy to carry safely on a motorbike.

Pack items properly and mark fragile items in the app. Riders may refuse a package that is unsafe, badly packed or does not match its description.

Problems with a delivery

  • Tell us about a lost, damaged or wrong delivery as soon as possible, and within 24 hours, with the order code and any photos. We will look into it with the rider and the in-app records.
  • Be respectful to riders. Abuse, threats or fraud can lead to your account being closed.
