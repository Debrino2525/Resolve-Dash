Motorbike delivery across Ghana

Fast local deliveries by verified riders.

GhanaDash connects independent motorbike riders with delivery orders anywhere in Ghana. Every rider is identity-checked, phone-verified and approved by our team before they can take an order.

How riders join

  1. Install the GhanaDash Rider app and sign in

    Use your Google account or an email address and password.

  2. Confirm your mobile number by SMS

    We text a one-time 6-digit code to your Ghana mobile number. Enter it in the app to prove the number is yours.

  3. Get approved

    Our operations team reviews your details before your account is activated.

  4. Start your free trial, then ride weekly

    Approved riders get a free trial, then dispatch access is a prepaid weekly subscription (GH₵50 per week), paid by mobile money or card through Paystack. Nothing renews automatically.

About our SMS verification codes

GhanaDash sends one-time passcodes (OTPs) by SMS from the sender ID GhanaDash only when you sign up in the GhanaDash Rider app and confirm your mobile number. A code is valid for 10 minutes and can be used once. We will never call or message you to ask for your code. Do not share it with anyone. If you received a code you did not request, you can ignore it, and contact us if it keeps happening.
